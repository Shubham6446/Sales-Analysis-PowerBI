# Suggested Power BI dashboard

## KPI cards
- Total Sales
- Total Orders
- Average Order Value
- Unique Products

## Charts
- Line chart: Order Date (Month) vs Total Sales
- Bar chart: Category vs Total Sales
- Column chart: Region vs Total Sales
- Table: Product and Total Sales

## Slicers
- Region
- Category
- Order Date

## Questions the dashboard should answer
1. Which category contributes the most sales?
2. Which region contributes the most sales?
3. How do sales change month by month?
4. Which five products generate the most sales?

Use the actual chart outputs to write your findings; do not invent business insights.
